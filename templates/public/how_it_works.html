How CivicPulse Works

A simple workflow connecting citizens, administrators, departments, and workers to resolve civic issues efficiently.

Complaint Resolution Process

1. Citizen Reports Issue

Citizens submit complaints by providing issue details, category, location, and supporting images.

2. Complaint Verification

The administration reviews submitted complaints and verifies the provided information.

3. Department Assignment

Verified complaints are assigned to the appropriate government department for further action.

4. Worker Allocation

Available workers are assigned based on complaint category, location, and priority level.

5. Issue Resolution

Workers resolve the reported issue and update the complaint status.

6. Citizen Feedback

Citizens provide feedback after resolution to improve service quality.
