About CivicPulse

A smart platform connecting citizens, administrators, and workers for efficient civic issue resolution.

What is CivicPulse?

CivicPulse is a Smart Civic Complaint Management System designed to simplify the process of reporting, tracking, and resolving public issues.

Citizens can report problems such as potholes, garbage overflow, drainage issues, street light failures, and water leakage through a centralized digital platform.

Our Vision

To create a transparent and responsive civic management ecosystem where citizens and government departments work together to improve urban services.

CivicPulse focuses on faster complaint resolution, better communication, and data-driven decision making.

Problems We Solve

📢 Easy Complaint Reporting

Citizens can quickly submit civic complaints without visiting government offices.

🔍 Transparent Tracking

Users can track complaint status from submission to final resolution.

⚡ Faster Resolution

Complaints are assigned to appropriate departments for efficient handling.

📊 Smart Management

Administrators can analyze complaint trends and improve city services.

Technology Behind CivicPulse

Frontend

HTML, CSS, JavaScript and responsive design for a user-friendly interface.

Backend

Flask framework handles application logic, routing, authentication and APIs.

Database

Oracle Database stores users, complaints, departments, workers and status history.

Intelligence Layer

Smart priority detection and duplicate complaint analysis improve complaint handling.

Benefits of CivicPulse

  • ✔ Reduces manual complaint management
  • ✔ Improves communication between citizens and departments
  • ✔ Provides real-time complaint status tracking
  • ✔ Helps authorities make data-driven decisions
  • ✔ Creates a cleaner and smarter community
