# CivicPulse - Project Problem Statement

## Project Title

**CivicPulse: Smart Civic Complaint Management System**

---

# Problem Statement

Many cities still rely on manual or inefficient methods for reporting and resolving civic issues such as potholes, garbage accumulation, drainage blockages, broken street lights, and water leakages. Citizens often face delays in registering complaints, tracking their status, and receiving timely updates. In many cases, complaints are duplicated, assigned incorrectly, or remain unresolved due to poor communication between citizens and municipal departments.

Existing systems generally lack transparency, centralized complaint management, automated prioritization, and proper tracking mechanisms. As a result, both citizens and government authorities experience difficulties in managing civic services efficiently.

---

# Proposed Solution

CivicPulse is a web-based Smart Civic Complaint Management System developed using Flask and Oracle Database. The system provides a centralized platform where citizens can report civic issues, upload supporting images, track complaint progress, and provide feedback after issue resolution.

The application enables administrators to verify complaints, assign them to the appropriate departments, monitor work progress, and maintain complete transparency throughout the complaint lifecycle.

---

# Objectives

- Provide an easy platform for citizens to report civic issues.
- Maintain a centralized database of complaints.
- Assign complaints to the appropriate departments.
- Enable administrators to monitor complaint status.
- Allow workers to update complaint progress.
- Improve transparency between citizens and municipal authorities.
- Reduce complaint resolution time.
- Generate useful analytics for decision-making.

---

# Scope of the Project

The system supports the complete complaint management workflow, including:

- Citizen Registration and Login

- Complaint Categorization
- Location-Based Complaint Reporting
- Complaint Status Tracking
- Department Assignment
- Worker Management
- Complaint Prioritization
- Feedback and Rating
